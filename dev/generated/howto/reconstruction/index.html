Wrap a Reconstruction in a TomoImage

A reconstruction ends with an array. This guide, for developers of reconstruction packages, turns it into a TomoImage that carries where its voxels lie, what its dimensions are and how it was made: an MRI reconstruction of an oblique volume with echoes and coils, and an MPI reconstruction with contrasts and a time series. Grids, Spaces and Transforms explains the conventions.

using TomoImages

What a scanner reports

Scanner metadata usually describes the reconstructed volume by its field of view, its matrix size, the position of its center and the directions of its axes. A GridGeometry needs the spacing, the center of the first voxel and the directions:

  • spacing = field of view ./ matrix size, where the field of view spans from the outer edge of the first voxel to the outer edge of the last. With gaps between slices, the spacing along the slices is the distance between slice centers, not the slice thickness;
  • origin = center - direction * (spacing .* (matrix size .- 1) ./ 2).

MRI: an oblique volume

An MRI reconstruction of 64 × 64 × 8 voxels over a field of view of 220 × 220 × 40 mm, three echoes and four receive coils. The volume is rotated by 20 degrees about the z axis of the patient space LPS; its read, phase and slice directions are the columns of D:

matrix = (64, 64, 8)
extent = (220.0, 220.0, 40.0)
center = [10.0, -5.0, 30.0]
dir_read, dir_phase, dir_slice = [cosd(20), sind(20), 0.0], [-sind(20), cosd(20), 0.0], [0.0, 0.0, 1.0]
D = hcat(dir_read, dir_phase, dir_slice)
voxel = extent ./ matrix
g = GridGeometry(matrix; spacing=voxel, origin=center - D * collect(voxel .* (matrix .- 1) ./ 2),
                 direction=D, space=LPS())
GridGeometry 64x64x8 in LPS (3D)
  spacing:   (3.4375, 3.4375, 5.0)
  origin:    (-54.71672294999158, -143.78546023895672, 12.5)
  direction: [0.9396926207859084 -0.3420201433256687 0.0; 0.3420201433256687 0.9396926207859084 0.0; 0.0 0.0 1.0]

The center of the grid is where the scanner put it:

fov_center(g) ≈ center
true

The reconstruction returns one complex value per voxel, echo and coil. The dimensions get the names of the MRI axes; the echo dimension holds the echo times, the coil dimension the coil numbers as labels. meta is free: the package reads none of it, and the keys here are an example.

reco = zeros(ComplexF32, 64, 64, 8, 3, 4)
img = TomoImage(reco, g;
                dims=(read=GridAxis(1), phase=GridAxis(2), slice=GridAxis(3),
                      echo=NumericAxis([5.0, 10.0, 15.0]; unit="ms"), coil=CategoricalAxis(1:4)),
                meta=(quantity="MR signal", unit="a.u.", method="direct reconstruction"))
summary(img)
"64×64×8×3×4 TomoImage{ComplexF32} in LPS with dims read, phase, slice, echo, coil"

A user of the image selects by name or by value, and every selection keeps its place: slice 4 has its center 2.5 mm below the center of the volume along the slice direction:

summary(selectvalue(img; echo=10.0, coil=2)), fov_center(geometry(img[slice=4])) ≈ center - 2.5 * dir_slice
("64×64×8 TomoImage{ComplexF32} in LPS with dims read, phase, slice", true)

MPI: contrasts and a time series

An MPI reconstruction is placed in the coordinates of its scanner. The package that reads the scanner data defines a space for it, a type of its own (see Define a Space):

struct MPIScanner <: AbstractSpace end

20 × 20 × 10 voxels over 40 × 40 × 20 mm, centered 5 mm from the center of the scanner along +z; two tracers and 50 frames, 21.5 ms apart. The grid dimensions sit between the contrast and the time dimension, in the order the reconstruction produces them:

matrix = (20, 20, 10)
voxel = (40.0, 40.0, 20.0) ./ matrix
g = GridGeometry(matrix; spacing=voxel, origin=[0.0, 0.0, 5.0] .- voxel .* (matrix .- 1) ./ 2, space=MPIScanner())
c = rand(Float32, 2, 20, 20, 10, 50)
mpi = TomoImage(c, g;
                dims=(contrast=CategoricalAxis(["tracer A", "tracer B"]),
                      x=GridAxis(1), y=GridAxis(2), z=GridAxis(3),
                      time=NumericAxis(0.0215 .* (0:49); unit="s")),
                meta=(quantity="concentration", unit="mmol/L"))
summary(mpi)
"2×20×20×10×50 TomoImage{Float32} in MPIScanner with dims contrast, x, y, z, time"

The same functions apply as for the MRI image. TomoImages.griddims gives the array dimensions of the grid axes:

summary(selectvalue(mpi; contrast="tracer B", time=0.5)), TomoImages.griddims(mpi)
("20×20×10 TomoImage{Float32} in MPIScanner with dims x, y, z", (2, 3, 4))

Checklist

  • The origin is the center of the first voxel. Convert from a center of the field of view as above.
  • Spacings are positive; a flipped axis is a direction column with the opposite sign.
  • The columns of the direction are orthonormal. Values read from files in Float32 are accepted.
  • A 2D slice in a 3D space has an origin of length 3 and a 3 × 2 direction.
  • The element type of the geometry follows the numbers given for spacing, origin and direction (integers give Float64).
  • The space says which coordinate system the positions are in: LPS or RAS where the scanner reports patient coordinates, a space of the package for scanner coordinates. Where the metadata relates the scanner to the patient, a method of transform_between makes that available.
  • Every dimension has a name; non-grid dimensions have coordinates with a unit (NumericAxis) or labels (CategoricalAxis).
  • The data may be any array, also on a GPU: the image wraps it, and parent(img) returns it as it is.
  • The metadata says what the values are and how they were computed.
