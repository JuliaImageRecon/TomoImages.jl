Related Packages

TomoImages sits between the packages that reconstruct images and the packages that analyze, store or show them. This page lists the packages around it and what each of them does.

Reconstruction

These packages compute images from measurements; their results are what TomoImages describes.

  • CTOperators.jl: projectors for iterative CT reconstruction and FDK, on CPUs and GPUs.
  • MRIReco.jl: MRI reconstruction, from k-space data with several echoes, coils and repetitions.
  • MPIReco.jl: reconstruction for magnetic particle imaging, of several tracers and many frames per second.
  • KomaMRI.jl: an MRI simulator. Its phantoms are point clouds of spins with tissue properties; it reconstructs through MRIReco.jl.
  • Sinograms.jl: CT geometries and filtered back projection.
  • AbstractImageReconstruction.jl: the framework of reconstruction plans that MPIReco.jl and MRIReco.jl build on.

Arrays with axes and metadata

These packages attach names, coordinates or metadata to arrays.

  • AxisArrays.jl: one named axis of values per dimension; integers are indices, values are selected with atvalue and intervals. Each axis is independent, so a grid cannot be rotated.
  • DimensionalData.jl: named dimensions with lookups, selectors (At, Near, Between), stacks of layers and many integrations. Rotated grids use a Transformed lookup.
  • Rasters.jl: builds on DimensionalData.jl and adds coordinate reference systems, reprojection and resampling for geospatial data: for maps, the coordinate system and the transforms between grids that TomoImages provides for tomographic images.
  • ImageCore.jl and the JuliaImages packages around it: traits such as pixelspacing and spacedirections for images, colors, and image processing. There is no trait for the origin of an image.
  • ImageGeoms.jl: the image grid of iterative reconstruction (dimensions, pixel sizes, offsets in pixels from the center, a support mask), with the conventions of the Michigan Image Reconstruction Toolbox.
  • MedImages.jl: 3D medical images with origin, spacing and direction, and patient metadata.

TomoImages differs from AxisArrays.jl and DimensionalData.jl in what it puts first: one geometry for all grid dimensions together (origin, spacing and a direction matrix, so rotated grids and slices are exact), the coordinate space as a type that is checked, transforms between spaces, and resampling between grids. Its other dimensions carry coordinates or labels and support selection by index and by value; the packages above add lookups, selectors and analysis along them.

Algorithms and formats

  • Interpolations.jl: the B-spline interpolation behind BSplineResampler.
  • KernelAbstractions.jl: the kernels of NearestResampler and LinearResampler, for CPUs and GPUs.
  • ImageTransformations.jl: warping images in index space, with CoordinateTransformations.jl for the transforms.
  • NIfTI.jl and DICOM.jl: reading and writing medical image files.

Conventions of Formats and Packages shows how the grids of DICOM, NIfTI and ImageGeoms.jl translate into grid geometries.
