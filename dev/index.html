TomoImages.jl

Placement, orientation and dimensions for tomographic images.

TomoImages.jl describes reconstructed images together with their place in space: the positions and orientation of their voxels, the coordinate system these are given in (a space), and the meaning of every dimension. Its types can be used by reconstruction packages to return their images and by packages that register, segment or combine images to read them, so that images from different scanners and modalities can be compared and resampled onto each other, on the CPU and on GPUs.

using TomoImages

# 64 × 64 × 32 voxels of 0.5 × 0.5 × 1 mm in LPS, the patient space with +x toward the patient's
# left, +y toward the back and +z toward the head; origin: the center of the first voxel
g = GridGeometry((64, 64, 32); spacing=(0.5, 0.5, 1.0), origin=(-15.75, -15.75, -15.5), space=LPS())
# the three array dimensions are the index axes 1 to 3 of the grid, named x, y and z
img = TomoImage(rand(Float32, 64, 64, 32), g; dims=(x=GridAxis(1), y=GridAxis(2), z=GridAxis(3)))
slice = img[z=16]                               # a 64 × 64 image that keeps its place in the volume
index_to_world(geometry(slice), (1, 1)), space(slice)
([-15.75, -15.75, -0.5], LPS())
coarse = GridGeometry((32, 32, 16); spacing=(1.0, 1.0, 2.0), origin=(-15.5, -15.5, -15.0), space=LPS())
summary(resample(img, coarse))                  # the image on a coarser grid, multilinear interpolation
"32×32×16 TomoImage{Float32} in LPS with dims x, y, z"

Background

A tomographic image shows the inside of an object, slice by slice or as a volume, without cutting it open (tomos: slice). It is not recorded directly but computed from measurements taken outside the object: computed tomography (CT) reconstructs it from X-ray projections, magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) from the signal of nuclear spins in k-space, magnetic particle imaging (MPI) from the response of magnetic nanoparticles injected as a tracer, emission tomography (PET, SPECT) from the decays of a radioactive tracer. Each reconstruction ends with an array, and an array knows only indices. It does not record how far apart its voxels are, whether the imaged volume was tilted against the scanner, whether its positions refer to the coordinates of one scanner, of another scanner or of the patient, or which of its dimensions are space and which are time, contrast (in MPI, one image per tracer), coil or echo. Two images whose numbers look alike may cover different parts of the body.

Every package that combines images, such as registration, segmentation, viewers or the overlay of an MPI image on a CT image, needs this information and has to keep it consistent through every slice, permutation and resampling. TomoImages keeps it with the image, much as packages for geospatial data such as Rasters.jl keep the coordinate system and placement of maps (see Related Packages). Lengths are plain numbers in one unit for all geometries of an application, millimeters by convention.

Scope

Grid geometries. An origin (the center of the first voxel), a spacing, a direction matrix for rotated and reflected grids, and the space they are given in. Any number of dimensions, and slices that keep their place in the space of their volume.

Spaces as types. A default space for work in a single coordinate system, the patient spaces LPS and RAS, and spaces that other packages define, for example for the coordinates of a scanner. Images and transforms in different spaces are only combined through a transform that connects them.

Images with named dimensions. A TomoImage wraps any AbstractArray, for example a GPU array, with its geometry, a name for every dimension, coordinates or labels for the dimensions that are not part of the grid (time, contrast, coil, echo), and metadata. Grid dimensions may sit anywhere among the others, as in MPI reconstructions with (contrast, x, y, z, time). Indexing, views, permutedims, reverse and broadcasting keep the description.

Transforms and resampling. Transforms between spaces are checked when they are composed or used. Resampling puts an image onto any other grid, through a transform where the spaces differ: nearest neighbor and multilinear interpolation as one KernelAbstractions kernel for CPUs and GPUs, B-splines with Interpolations.jl, and resamplers of other packages. Integer images such as label maps are resampled with the nearest neighbor by default, so labels are not averaged.

Few dependencies and custom components. The package has few dependencies, so that reconstruction packages can use it to return their images, and other packages can add spaces, resamplers and image types without changing TomoImages.

Not in scope. Reconstruction, registration, segmentation, reading scanner files and displaying images are tasks of other packages, which exchange their results through these types.
